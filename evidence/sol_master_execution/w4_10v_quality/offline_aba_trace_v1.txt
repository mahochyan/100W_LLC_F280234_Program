Design boundary
- Observer only: no PWM write, threshold change, enable request, TZ clear, or
  protection change.
- One-shot arm is consumed when a valid protected-Burst hold request starts.
- 128-entry circular trace at 5 ms records VOUT raw, packet cycles, and packet
  count. Baseline is 200 ms beginning at 500 ms.
- Detection begins at 5 s and requires two consecutive 20 ms blocks with a
  >=12.5% change in the commanded direction.
- Demand index = (cycles per 5 ms * average cycles per packet) / 2. This uses
  both switching duty and packet depth; it does not affect the controller.
- Captured quality window is 240 ms from the first changed block: instantaneous
  VOUT must remain raw1182..1306 (+/-5%), and the 20 ms moving average must
  enter raw1215..1265 (+/-2%) within 100 ms and remain there.
- A non-complete observer at the firmware-owned run end is frozen as FAIL.
