ROOT_CAUSE
V7 passed 500 ms, 2 s, and 10 s but its 60 s request safely aborted at
2.49724 s. The final packet started raw997, ran 128 cycles through DB90,
ended raw1001, and peaked raw1005. DB90 therefore has positive but insufficient
low-output recovery margin before the unchanged three-sample floor stop.

V8_CHANGE
- Each packet starts deterministically at TBPRD239/DB110.
- DB110 is held for 15 complete cycles, then DBRED/DBFED fall by five only
  after completed cycles 15, 25, 35, ..., 125.
- A full 128-cycle packet reaches DB50 after cycle125; period and CMPA remain
  fixed at TBPRD239/CMPA120 and the later DB36 phase is never entered.
- This exactly mirrors a strict prefix of the accelerated Profile-C Phase-A
  cadence proven during every successful initial charge.
- The integrated effective-pulse authority of a full packet is approximately
  31% above V7, while the early-cycle ramp is slower than V7.

UNCHANGED
128-cycle/512us packet cap; 50% aggregate cap; per-cycle target raw1260 and
hard raw1300; three-sample raw1000 undersupply; 40us minimum OFF; deterministic
first edge; private one-call DB authorization; Comparator/TZ/DAC/GPIO15;
production envelope; legacy 11V max15; no automatic retry.
