W3_10V_BURST_HOLD_V4 bounded hold-energy authority qualification
Qualified at: 2026-09-05T00:20:12+08:00
Source commit: eeb9f756b92d26a925efbb2874444a5d0393940f

MEASURED_ROOT_CAUSE
V3 ran 32235 identical 250kHz/DB110 cycles during 34438 x 20us hold ticks.
PWM-active time = 32235 / 250000 = 0.12894s.
Observed active fraction = 0.12894 / 0.68876 = 18.72%.
The old 2s aggregate cap permitted only 50000 / 250000 = 0.2s, exactly
10% of the requested duration. Every V3 packet also reached its 15-cycle cap.
The old bounded authority was therefore below already-observed load demand.

V4_CHANGE
- Keep the already exercised 250kHz/TBPRD239/DB110 cycle unchanged.
- W3-only per-packet ceiling: 64 cycles = 256us maximum active interval.
- W3-only aggregate ceiling: 50% of each selected duration:
  500ms=62500, 2s=250000, 10s=1250000, 60s=7500000 cycles.
- Legacy 11V remains at its original 15-cycle packet ceiling and original
  aggregate caps.
- Every fresh PWM-cycle sample still stops at target raw1260 and hard-stops at
  raw1300; every packet remains comparator/TZ protected.

SAFETY_RATIONALE
V3 proved 2126 deterministic cold starts and 32235 individual 250kHz/DB110
cycles without a hardware/active-window trip. V4 does not increase per-cycle
voltage, frequency, duty command, or current threshold. It only permits more
of those same cycles before the software packet ceiling, while retaining a
finite 256us packet limit, 40us minimum off time, a 50% aggregate time limit,
per-cycle voltage termination, raw1300 hard stop, and Comparator/TZ authority.
The previously proven Profile-C charge completed 419 cycles and is unchanged.

UNCHANGED
Deterministic first-edge sequence; Comparator/TZ/DAC threshold; GPIO15
qualification; frequency; DB; W3 voltage thresholds; ADC paths; production
envelope; public enable isolation; no automatic retry.
